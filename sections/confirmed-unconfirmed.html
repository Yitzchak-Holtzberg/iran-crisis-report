RETALIATION — BAHRAIN NSA: DIRECT MISSILE HIT CONFIRMED (Al Jazeera / Middle East Eye)
Iran's most significant confirmed retaliation strike: a missile impacted a tower block adjacent to NSA Bahrain — US 5th Fleet HQ in Manama. Social media videos confirm the direct impact; fires and property damage confirmed. Bahraini authorities described it as a "treacherous attack." Civil defense forces deployed for rescue operations in affected residential districts. — Sources: Al Jazeera, Middle East Eye, US News & World Report, Feb 28
RETALIATION — UAE: 1 CIVILIAN KILLED, 4 INJURED (US News / Al Jazeera)
One civilian was killed by falling missile debris in Abu Dhabi following Iranian missile strikes targeting the UAE. Four others were injured. A building fire broke out on Palm Jumeirah; large plumes of smoke were visible across the city. Missiles were intercepted but debris caused casualties. — Sources: US News & World Report, Al Jazeera, Feb 28
STRIKES — FORDOW CONFIRMED HIT; SURFACE INFRASTRUCTURE DESTROYED (Times Now / CBS)
The Fordow uranium enrichment plant (buried ~90m under a mountain near Qom) was struck in Operation Epic Fury. Key access routes and surface infrastructure are confirmed destroyed via satellite imagery. Whether the underground centrifuge halls were penetrated remains unconfirmed — the facility is hardened beyond standard bunker-buster specifications. — Sources: Times Now Israel targets list, CBS News map, Feb 28
STRIKES — IRGC COMMAND CENTERS IN TEHRAN CONFIRMED HIT (The War Zone / IDF)
Multiple IRGC command-and-control buildings in Tehran were confirmed struck, per IDF imagery: Alborz Corps HQ (Tehran garrison), Thar-Allah Command Centre (capital security), and Sayyed al-Shuhada Corps (national homeland defense). Ministry of Defense and Ministry of Intelligence also struck. — Sources: The War Zone, Times Now, Kurdistan24, Feb 28

Whether US GBU-57 bunker-busters successfully penetrated and destroyed the "Pickaxe Mountain" deep facility and Fordow's underground centrifuge halls remains unconfirmed. US officials claim "significant damage" but no independent BDA has been released. — Sources: CSIS analysis; BDA pending, Feb 28
LEADERSHIP — SUPREME LEADER KHAMENEI STATUS UNVERIFIED
Satellite imagery shows smoke from the vicinity of Supreme Leader Khamenei's Niavaran/Jamaran compound in Tehran. Some reports suggest the residence area was struck. Whether Khamenei was killed, injured, or escaped is entirely unconfirmed. US and Israel have made no claim of a leadership kill. Iranian state media has not issued any statement from or about the Supreme Leader. — Sources: Newsweek, Wikipedia (2026 strikes) — Unverified, Feb 28
