NUCLEAR — NATANZ CASCADE HALLS A AND B SEVERELY DAMAGED CONFIRMED MAR 25
The IAEA’s Mar 25 technical assessment confirmed Natanz centrifuge cascade halls A and B are “severely damaged and unlikely to be operational for 6–18 months.” No radiation release detected at any monitoring station. Fordow status remains unverified. — Sources: IAEA, Mar 25; Reuters, Mar 25
MARITIME — FIRST HORMUZ CONVOY TRANSITS WITHOUT INCIDENT CONFIRMED MAR 27
The first US Navy-escorted convoy of 12 tankers transited the Strait of Hormuz on Mar 27 without incident. Iran’s Foreign Ministry stated Iran would “not fire on neutral-flagged commercial vessels operating under international law.” — Sources: Bloomberg; Reuters, Mar 27
NUCLEAR — HEU FRAMEWORK DRAFT UNDER ACTIVE NEGOTIATION CONFIRMED MAR 27
Both US and Iranian sides confirmed that a preliminary HEU framework document — covering transfer of ~440 kg of 60% HEU to a third country, enrichment freeze, and Hormuz reopening timeline — is under active negotiation in Oman. — Sources: Axios; NYT; Reuters, Mar 27
US CASUALTIES — 17 KIA INCLUDING FOUR IN AL-UDEID DRONE ATTACK CONFIRMED MAR 28
Pentagon confirmed four US service members killed in a drone attack on the Al-Udeid perimeter on Mar 28, raising the total US KIA to 17 since Feb 28. — Sources: Pentagon; AP, Mar 28
HORMUZ — MINE-CLEARING OPERATIONS UNDERWAY; TWO MINES DETONATED CONFIRMED MAR 26
US Navy mine-countermeasures vessels safely detonated two influence mines in the western approaches to the Strait of Hormuz on Mar 25–26. Additional MCM sorties are ongoing. — Sources: USNI News; Reuters, Mar 26
DIPLOMACY — IRAN-US BACK-CHANNEL VIA OMAN NOW FORMAL TRACK CONFIRMED MAR 27
What began as reported back-channel MOIS contacts (Mar 3–4, Tier-5 sources) has been fully confirmed as a formal US–Iran negotiating channel mediated by Oman. Both sides have publicly acknowledged the Oman talks and the production of a preliminary HEU framework document. The channel is the most advanced diplomatic engagement between Washington and Tehran since the conflict began. — Sources: Axios; Reuters; both governments, Mar 25–29
UK — RAF FAIRFORD NOW HOSTS U.S. B‑1B LANCERS FOR IRAN OPERATIONS CONFIRMED MAR 9
The UK Ministry of Defence confirmed U.S. B‑1B bombers arrived at RAF Fairford for "specific defensive operations to prevent Iran firing at NATO assets". Indicates limited basing access despite UK's non‑participant stance in strikes. — Sources: Defense News, Mar 9; Military Times, Mar 9
GULF — IRANIAN DRONE DAMAGES BAHRAIN DESALINATION PLANT CONFIRMED MAR 8
Bahrain reported damage to a desalination plant from an Iranian drone strike amid Tehran’s widening retaliation. — Sources: AP, Mar 8
MARITIME — IRANIAN NAVY DESTROYED CONFIRMED MAR 10
CENTCOM's 10-day operational summary confirmed 50+ Iranian warships destroyed and over 5,000 targets struck. The Iranian navy is now operationally irrelevant. — Sources: CENTCOM, Middle East Monitor, Mar 10
CASUALTY FIGURES — 4,300 KILLED IN FIRST 10 DAYS UPDATED MAR 10
Hengaw Organisation for Human Rights documented 4,300 killed in the first 10 days — 3,900 military (mostly IRGC, army, air force) and 390 civilians (9.6%), including schoolchildren in Hormozgan. Iran's Deputy Health Minister separately cited 1,255 killed and 12,000 wounded (Mar 9). IDF claims 1,900+ Iranian soldiers killed. Pentagon revealed ~140 US troops wounded, 8 severely. Figures vary by source. — Sources: Hengaw, Al Jazeera, CNN, Axios, Mar 9–10
KHARG ISLAND — U.S. STRIKES MILITARY SITES (MAR 14)
U.S. forces struck military targets on Kharg Island, the primary oil export hub, in a strike package on Mar 14. U.S. officials said the strikes were aimed at military installations and not intended to damage oil-handling infrastructure; President Trump warned further attacks on shipping could bring strikes on Iran's energy facilities. — Source: AP, Mar 14; Reuters, Mar 14
INTELLIGENCE — IRAN INTELLIGENCE MINISTER KHATIB CONFIRMED KILLED CONFIRMED MAR 18–19
Multiple Tier 1–2 outlets confirmed the killing of Iran's intelligence minister Esmaeil Khatib in an Israeli airstrike, removing the head of MOIS/VAJA. Israeli Defence Minister Katz confirmed the operation and said "significant surprises" lie ahead. Iran's armed forces chief vowed a "decisive and regrettable" response. Khatib was a senior figure in Iran's espionage and internal security apparatus. — Sources: New York Times, Mar 19; BBC, Mar 19; Reuters, Mar 18
ENERGY — QATAR RAS LAFFAN LNG HUB SUSTAINS EXTENSIVE DAMAGE FROM IRANIAN STRIKES CONFIRMED MAR 19
QatarEnergy confirmed "extensive damage" to additional LNG sites at Ras Laffan following Iranian missile strikes, triggering major fires. Qatar PM Al Thani said the attack was "clear proof" Iran is not just targeting US interests and that Qatar retains the right to respond under international law. European natural gas futures surged 30–35%; Brent crude briefly surpassed $119/bbl. — Sources: Bloomberg, Mar 19; Reuters, Mar 19
MILITARY — ISRAEL STRIKES IRAN'S SOUTH PARS GAS FIELD, COORDINATED WITH US CONFIRMED MAR 19
Three Israeli officials confirmed to Reuters that Israel struck Iran's South Pars gas field in coordination with the US and that the strike is "not likely to be repeated." Trump confirmed he asked Netanyahu not to strike South Pars again, while warning Iran the field could be "destroyed" if attacks on Qatar continue. — Sources: Reuters, Mar 19; Hindustan Times, Mar 19
HUMAN COST — IRANIAN MISSILES KILL PALESTINIANS IN WEST BANK — FIRST DEADLY IRAN STRIKES THERE CONFIRMED MAR 18–19
The Palestinian Red Crescent confirmed four Palestinian women were killed in Iranian missile strikes in the occupied West Bank — the first deadly Iranian attacks on the West Bank, and the first to kill Palestinians, in the war. NUCLEAR — FORDOW UNDERGROUND HALLS STATUS UNKNOWN ? UNCONFIRMED
The IAEA’s Mar 25 assessment confirmed Natanz cascade halls A and B are “severely damaged” but said Fordow status remains “unverified due to access restrictions.” Whether the deep underground Fordow facility was effectively struck by GBU-57 MOPs — or whether intact centrifuge capacity survives below ground — remains the most critical open intelligence question. What confirmation would look like: IAEA physical access to Fordow or independent technical assessment. — Source: IAEA, Mar 25
NEGOTIATIONS — HEU TRANSFER THIRD COUNTRY: KAZAKHSTAN AGREED? ? UNCONFIRMED
Multiple outlets cite Kazakhstan as the named candidate to receive Iran’s 60% HEU stockpile under the Oman framework draft. Kazakhstan’s government has not officially confirmed willingness to accept the material. What confirmation would look like: An official Kazakhstani government statement or Tier 1–2 confirmation from Astana. — Sources: Axios, Mar 27; unnamed US official via NYT, Mar 27

What confirmation would look like: Authenticated statement from Mojtaba or Tier 1–2 reporting on his specific authorization. — Sources: Reuters, Mar 25–29; no confirmed sightings

These claims are not independently verified — Iran continues firing ballistic missiles (multiple salvos fired Mar 18–19), suggesting substantial residual stockpiles; whether production capacity is durably degraded remains unassessed.
