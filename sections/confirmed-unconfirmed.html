MARITIME — IRANIAN NAVY DESTROYED CONFIRMED MAR 7
Previously listed as unconfirmed. CENTCOM's week-one operational summary confirmed 43 Iranian warships destroyed and over 3,000 targets struck. While "total fleet destruction" remains an overstatement, the Iranian navy is now operationally irrelevant. — Sources: CENTCOM, AP, Mar 7
HORMUZ — STRAIT FUNCTIONALLY CLOSED CONFIRMED MAR 7
Previously listed as IRGC claim vs operational reality. Now confirmed: only 4 commercial transits in 24 hours, IRGC drone strikes on tankers Prima and Louise P, insurers pulled war-risk cover, ~20% of global oil supply offline. The Strait is not physically blocked but is functionally closed to commercial shipping. — Sources: Reuters, Euronews, CNBC, Mar 7
NUCLEAR — IAEA CONFIRMS NATANZ DAMAGE; NO RADIATION RELEASE CONFIRMED MAR 3
IAEA confirmed buildings at Natanz sustained damage and stated "no radiological consequence expected." The IAEA is seeking access for full verification. Iran's denial that "civilian nuclear infrastructure" was targeted is contradicted by the IAEA's own report that safeguarded facilities were damaged. — Sources: IAEA, Al Jazeera, Mar 3
CASUALTY FIGURES — 1,332+ KILLED INCLUDING 181 CHILDREN UPDATED MAR 7
The disputed figure of 555 killed (Al Jazeera, Mar 2) has been superseded. After one week of strikes, the toll has risen to 1,332+ killed including at least 181 children (UNICEF). The Iranian Red Crescent reported 6,668 civilian sites targeted. All figures remain preliminary due to access restrictions. — Sources: Reuters, Gulf News, UNICEF, AP, Mar 7

NUCLEAR — DEEP BUNKER BDA: FORDOW UNDERGROUND HALLS BDA PENDING
Whether US GBU-57 Massive Ordnance Penetrators successfully destroyed Fordow's underground centrifuge halls remains the most critical intelligence question of the campaign. These facilities are at 80–100m depth — at or beyond the weapon's effective range. Commercial satellite imagery shows crater signatures near entry shafts, but subsurface destruction is not assessable from surface imagery. A US intelligence assessment leaked to CSIS indicates strikes likely only set the programme back by months, not years — echoing the June 2025 assessment. Iran was observed sealing underground facility entrances at Isfahan with dump trucks and heavy equipment, suggesting some infrastructure survived. Whether Iran's nuclear programme was meaningfully degraded remains disputed. — Sources: CSIS Nuclear Network; FDD analysis, Mar 5; Reuters, Mar 2; BDA pending

Multiple reports say the CIA is arming Kurdish militias (KDPI/PJAK) to foment an internal insurgency. No Tier 1–2 confirmation exists. What confirmation would look like: on-record US acknowledgement, congressional notification, or visual evidence of weapons transfers. — Sources: CNN (Tier-4), Mar 3–5; not wire-confirmed
INTELLIGENCE — IRAN MOIS BACK-CHANNEL TO US ? UNCONFIRMED — TIER-5

Key ambiguity: unclear if this represents institutional policy approved by the Mojtaba junta, a factional move by MOIS elements, or fabrication. No Tier 1–2 confirmation. — Sources: Iran International, Al Arabiya (Tier-5), Mar 3–4
ECONOMIC — KHARG ISLAND OIL TERMINAL STRUCK NOT VERIFIED
Strike maps showed Kharg Island — handling ~90% of Iran's crude exports — as a reported target. No CENTCOM or independent visual confirmation. Given that oil depot strikes are now confirmed (Mar 7), a Kharg strike remains plausible but unverified. If confirmed, it would cut Iranian oil revenue by 90%. — Sources: Newsweek, Mar 1; CENTCOM silent; imagery pending
REGIONAL — IRANIAN STRIKE ON CYPRUS (RAF AKROTIRI) NOT CONFIRMED
Social media reports indicated possible Iranian missile activity targeting RAF Akrotiri in Cyprus. Not confirmed by UK MoD, Cyprus government, or CENTCOM. If verified, it would mark the first Iranian strike on a NATO-adjacent UK sovereign territory. Note: with the confirmed Turkish strike (Mar 4), Iran has already demonstrated willingness to hit near NATO assets. — Sources: Social media; UK MoD: no comment
COALITION — SAUDI ARABIA & QATAR AS ACTIVE COMBATANTS UNCONFIRMED
Reports stated Saudi Arabia and Qatar formally joined as co-belligerents. Both condemned Iranian attacks and activated air defenses, but no formal declaration of co-belligerency has been confirmed. Iran's apology to Gulf neighbors (Mar 7) suggests Tehran wants to avoid triggering formal Gulf state entry into the war. — Sources: Reuters, Atlantic Council, Mar 3–7
INTELLIGENCE — RUSSIA PROVIDING TARGETING DATA TO IRAN ? UNCONFIRMED

If confirmed, this would represent a significant escalation in Russian involvement and could trigger a diplomatic confrontation between Washington and Moscow. Not confirmed by US officials, CENTCOM, or Pentagon. — Sources: Euronews, Mar 7 — not independently verified

MARITIME — IRAN CLAIMS STRIKE ON USS ABRAHAM LINCOLN
Iranian state media claimed a strike on the USS Abraham Lincoln. US officials denied the carrier was hit and said it remains operational. — Sources: Naval-Technology, Mar 2; US officials
INTELLIGENCE — TRUMP CLAIMS IRAN BUILDING MISSILES TO HIT US
Trump asserted Iran is developing ICBMs capable of striking the United States. US intelligence officials found no evidence. DIA and CIA analysts assess Iran's ICBM capability as nonexistent. Iran's longest-range missiles are designed for regional reach only. — Sources: CNN, Feb 28; contradicted by US intel community
MOJTABA KHAMENEI APPOINTMENT — LEGITIMACY CONTESTED
The Assembly of Experts vote naming Mojtaba as Supreme Leader is confirmed to have occurred but its legitimacy is contested by opposition figures, reformist clerics, and Western governments. The quorum and independence of the session cannot be verified. The US and EU have not recognised the appointment. — Sources: Reuters, AP, Al Jazeera, Mar 3–6
