BREAKING — Khamenei Reported Dead (Feb 28–Mar 1, 2026)

Iranian state media reported that Supreme Leader Ayatollah Ali Khamenei is dead, a potentially regime‑defining development amid ongoing US‑Israeli strikes. Israeli officials publicly claimed he was killed during the strikes, a claim not independently verified as communications remain disrupted. — Sources: AP, Reuters, Al Jazeera, Feb 28–Mar 1

☠ Crisis 1: The January Massacre

7,015+ confirmed dead (HRANA) — 36,500+ by Iran International — the deadliest protest massacre in modern history. Protests spread to 203 cities across all 31 provinces following live-ammunition orders on January 8–9, 2026. At least 30 people, including children, now face execution for participation.

✊ Crisis 2: The Student Uprising

Students across all 31 provinces chant "Death to the dictator" and — for the first time in 46 years — chants calling for Reza Pahlavi. 410+ arrested since Feb 21. Sharif University hacked and renamed to its pre-revolution "Aryamehr" name.
