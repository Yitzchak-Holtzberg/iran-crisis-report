Inside Iran: A Regime Under Siege

Eight converging crises threatening the Islamic Republic
Critical
CONFIRMED — Khamenei Dead; Operation Epic Fury Day 2 (Mar 1, 2026)

Iranian state media and multiple officials confirmed the death of Supreme Leader Ayatollah Ali Khamenei after U.S.–Israeli strikes. The IRGC succession crisis is now active as competing factions move to consolidate control over the leaderless state. Simultaneously, Operation Epic Fury strikes entered Day 2 with Iran launching ~170 ballistic missiles in retaliation. The rial has fallen to ~1.7M/USD and communications are again effectively severed. — Sources: Reuters, AP, Al Jazeera, Mar 1

☠ Crisis 1: The January Massacre

7,015+ confirmed dead (HRANA) — 36,500+ by Iran International — the deadliest protest massacre in modern history. Protests spread to 203 cities across all 31 provinces following live-ammunition orders on January 8–9, 2026. At least 30 people, including children, now face execution for participation.

✊ Crisis 2: The Student Uprising

Now into Day 9 (March 1) — students across all 31 provinces chant "Death to the dictator" and — for the first time in 46 years — chants calling for Reza Pahlavi. 410+ arrested since Feb 21. News of Khamenei's death has sparked spontaneous street celebrations in several cities despite ongoing military strikes.
