Iran: Strike Damage Assessment

Operation Epic Fury — confirmed and assessed damage to Iranian targets (Feb 28–Mar 22, 2026)
Critical
Mar 22 — Wide‑scale strikes reported across Iran; Natanz hit; IAEA investigating

The latest wave of strikes on Mar 22 targeted missile production and defence‑industrial sites across Iran. Regional reporting and Iranian state outlets described damage at the Natanz enrichment complex; the IAEA said it is investigating and reported no detected rise in radiation levels outside the facility. Iranian ballistic missiles struck towns near Dimona and Arad, causing dozens of injuries in southern Israel. CENTCOM and allied statements describe renewed, intense targeting of regime military infrastructure. — Source: IAEA; Reuters; Al Jazeera; New York Times, Mar 22

Mar 15 — UN refugee agency: Millions displaced inside Iran; humanitarian needs surge

The UN refugee agency and humanitarian observers report a major internal displacement crisis in Iran, with estimates of roughly 3.2 million people displaced inside the country since the start of large-scale strikes. Aid organisations warn of urgent needs for shelter, medical care, and safe corridors. — Source: Los Angeles Times (Tier‑4, citing UNHCR), Mar 15

Mar 15 — Diplomatic push to halt fighting stalled; U.S. rebuffs allied ceasefire initiatives

Multiple reports indicate allied efforts to open immediate ceasefire talks have been rebuffed by the U.S. administration, while Iran insists all strikes must end before negotiations. Regional mediators (Turkey, Oman, Egypt) continue quiet diplomacy but say a breakthrough is not yet in sight. — Sources: Haaretz (Tier‑4), Mar 15; AP (Tier‑2), Mar 14

Mar 14 — US Strikes Military Sites on Kharg Island; Oil Infrastructure Spared for Now

The U.S. military struck military targets on Kharg Island, a strategic node for Iran's oil export network; officials said oil export terminals were deliberately avoided. Iranian outlets disputed the extent of damage. Global supply-risk concerns intensified. — Sources: AP (Tier‑2), Mar 14; Reuters (Tier‑2), Mar 14

Mar 12 — Fresh Iranian Attacks on Shipping; Oil Above $100 / School Strike Under Investigation

Iran launched new missile and drone attacks on Gulf shipping and terminals (Mar 11–12), with tankers struck in Iraqi waters and impacts near Dubai and Bahrain. Oil climbed above $100/barrel. Separately, the U.S. military opened an investigation into a strike that hit an elementary school in Iran, reportedly killing dozens of children; Congress is pressing for details. — Sources: Reuters (Tier‑2), Mar 12; Bloomberg (Tier‑2), Mar 12; NBC (Tier‑4), Mar 12; AP (Tier‑2), Mar 12

Mar 10 — Israel Announces New Wide-Scale Waves Across Iran

Israel said it carried out several wide-scale waves of strikes across Iran overnight, focusing on IRGC long-range missile launch sites and regime infrastructure. Crowds rallied in Tehran in support of the new Supreme Leader as strikes continued. — Sources: BBC (Tier‑4), Mar 10; CNN live (Tier‑4), Mar 9

International bodies note broad discrepancies and limited access for independent verification.
