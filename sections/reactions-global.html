International reactions to Operation Epic Fury — Mar 1–15, 2026
Elevated
Mar 10 — US Senate Democrats Demand Public Hearings on Iran War

A group of Democratic senators called for immediate public hearings, citing concerns over strategy, civilian harm, and troop protection. — Source: Reuters (Tier‑2), Mar 9–10

Mar 9 — Trump: 'Nowhere Near' Deciding to Send Troops Into Iran

President Trump said he is "nowhere near" deciding whether to deploy US troops into Iran to secure enriched uranium stockpiles, downplaying speculation of imminent ground operations. — Source: Reuters (Tier‑2), Mar 9

🇺🇸 United States

President Trump confirmed "major combat operations" against Iran, framing it as a last resort after talks failed. CENTCOM named the campaign Operation Epic Fury. The US ordered non-emergency staff to depart Saudi Arabia and deployed additional Marine/naval forces (USS Tripoli + MEU, B-2 rotations). — Sources: Reuters (Tier‑2), Mar 1; CNBC (Tier‑4), Mar 9; Axios (Tier‑4), Mar 13

US Domestic — Texas Shooting (Iran-Linked Probe)

A shooting near Joint Base San Antonio left multiple casualties (Mar 1). FBI leads the investigation; suspected Iran-linked connections consistent with pre-existing threat warnings. Force protection raised nationwide. — Sources: FBI (Tier‑1), DHS (Tier‑1), Mar 1

Major Powers & UN Security Council

  • 🇷🇺 Russia — Condemned the operation as "a gross violation of international law"; called emergency UNSC session warning of "unpredictable consequences." — Reuters (Tier‑2), Mar 1
  • 🇨🇳 China — Called for immediate cessation of military activities and respect for Iranian sovereignty; aligned with Russia on UNSC session. — Reuters (Tier‑2), Mar 1
  • 🇫🇷 France & 🇩🇪 Germany — Called for maximum restraint; condemned Iranian retaliation on Gulf states. EU convened emergency foreign affairs council; evacuations began. France deploying carrier Charles de Gaulle (R91) with Rafale M air wing for maritime security. — Reuters (Tier‑2), Mar 1–2; French MOD (Tier‑1), Mar 1

🇧🇲 NATO & Regional Strikes

  • 🇹🇷 Turkey — NATO Member Struck (Mar 4): Iranian BMs hit Turkish soil near İncirlik Air Base — first direct Iranian strike on a NATO member. Erdoğan condemned the attack; NATO invoked Article 4 consultations. Turkey recalled its ambassador to Tehran, closed airspace to mission-related overflights, and reserved its right of self-defense. — Reuters, Mar 4
  • 🇦🇿 Azerbaijan — Nakhchivan Struck (Mar 5): Iranian drones hit an airport and landed near a school in Azerbaijan's Nakhchivan exclave. Azerbaijan withdrew diplomats and promised a military response. Iran denied responsibility (claimed Israeli false flag). — Euronews (Tier‑4), Mar 5
  • 🇬🇧 United Kingdom: Initially defensive-only posture; by Mar 7 PM Starmer approved use of British bases for limited strikes on Iran's missile capabilities. HMS Diamond (Type 45) remains forward-deployed. Sky Sabre air defense offered to Gulf partners; Typhoon CAP offered; Royal Navy assets supporting humanitarian corridors. — UK FCDO (Tier‑1), UK MoD (Tier‑1), Reuters (Tier‑2), Mar 1–7
  • 🇨🇦 Canada: Calibrated, allied-aligned response — support for preventing a nuclear-armed Iran, calls for civilian protection, no direct military engagement. — Atlantic Council (Tier‑3), Mar 8
Energy & Shipping Markets — Updated Mar 15

Shipping through the Strait of Hormuz remains severely disrupted into week three. Kharg Island strikes and ongoing maritime attacks have kept Brent crude above $100/barrel. Governments are discussing naval escorts and insurance backstops. Reports indicate Iranian officials may be exploring conditional re-opening tied to non-US/Israeli vessel restrictions or alternative settlement mechanisms. — Sources: Reuters (Tier‑2), Mar 12–14; Bloomberg (Tier‑2), Mar 12; AP (Tier‑2), Mar 14; NDTV (Tier‑4), Mar 15

Regional Mediation Efforts — Turkey, Oman, Egypt

Turkey, Oman, and Egypt are pressing for a diplomatic end to the fighting via quiet back-channel talks with Tehran and regional capitals. Mediation is active but constrained by preconditions on both sides; no public ceasefire agreement has been reached. — Sources: Haaretz (Tier‑4), Mar 15; AP (Tier‑2), Mar 14
