The IAEA is looking into Iran's report that its Natanz enrichment site was struck again; Iranian authorities said there was no radioactive leakage. The development increases nuclear risk and complicates verification planning for any post-conflict arrangements. — Source: Washington Post / NBC News, Mar 21

The move is intended to blunt the market shock from Strait of Hormuz disruptions but also gives Tehran limited revenue access while the conflict continues. — Source: Reuters / New York Times, Mar 20

Saudi Arabia declared the Iranian military attache and three embassy staff persona non grata and ordered them to leave within 24 hours, citing repeated Iranian attacks on Saudi territory. The move signals widening regional diplomatic rupture. — Source: Reuters, Mar 21

Iran launched heavy retaliatory strikes after Larijani's killing. — Sources: BBC, Mar 18–19; Haaretz, Mar 17; NYT, Mar 18

Failure to pay is the single clearest financial-collapse signal and the most likely trigger for a first general-rank defection. All scenarios hinge on what happens in the next 24 hours. — Sources: Jerusalem Post, Mar 11; Polemology, Mar 2026
