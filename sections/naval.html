Allied navies are re-assessing convoy and escort postures amid heightened missile and asymmetric threat activity. — Source: Reuters, Mar 22

Mar 21 — Israeli strikes in Iran and Lebanon increase regional maritime risk

The Israeli military reported strikes on targets in Tehran and said it was striking Hezbollah targets in Beirut early today; these actions, together with Iranian reprisals, have raised immediate operational risk for merchant shipping and naval operations across the Gulf and Levant. Allied navies are reassessing convoy and escort posture as maritime insurance and routing disruption continue. — Source: Reuters, Mar 21

U.S. temporary oil sanction waiver — brief maritime implications

The U.S. issued a 30-day waiver permitting the sale of Iranian oil already at sea to ease global energy pressures; the move could shift tanker routing and commercial naval escorts in the short term even as the Strait of Hormuz remains operationally risky. — Source: Reuters, Mar 20
