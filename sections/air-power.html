Air Power: The Arsenal

160+ aircraft surged; B‑2 stealth bombers and a squadron of F‑35s deployed; ≈2,500 Marines deployed — largest aerial/naval build‑up (Mar 14)
Bomber Task Force forward-deploys to RAF Fairford (Mar 9–10)

B-1B Lancers arrived at RAF Fairford, with B-52H Stratofortress aircraft joining to support Operation Epic Fury. The UK MoD confirmed U.S. use of Fairford for "specific defensive operations to prevent Iran firing missiles into the region". CENTCOM-released footage and briefings highlighted precision bomber strikes on Iranian missile infrastructure in the opening phase. — Sources: Defense News, Mar 9; Military Times, Mar 9; Defense News Weekly, Mar 10

B-2 and Additional Long-Range Assets Deployed to Theater (Mar 13–14)

U.S. strategic assets including elements of the B-2 stealth bomber force and additional long-range tankers and support aircraft have been forward-deployed to sustain deep‑strike and precision options. These moves increase the ability to hit hardened or deeply buried Iranian targets from stand-off ranges. — Source: USNI News; The Jerusalem Post/Defense reporting, Mar 13–14

Amphibious & Marine Air Integration — Tripoli & MEU Adds Airlift Options (Mar 13)

The arrival of amphibious shipping and Marine Expeditionary Units (MV-22s, F-35Bs where embarked) provides the U.S. joint force with sea-based airlift, short‑field strike, and forcible-entry options — increasing flexible targeting choices without immediate reliance on host-nation basing. — Source: USNI News; Axios, Mar 13

NATO Air Defenses Intercept Iranian Missile Over Turkey (Mar 9)

Turkish officials and regional outlets reported that NATO-integrated air defenses intercepted an Iranian ballistic missile over Turkey amid ongoing barrages. Ankara reiterated it will not allow its airspace to be used for strikes against Iran. (Tier‑4 sourcing; not yet wire-confirmed) — Sources: Ynetnews, Mar 9; Axios, Mar 9

Air Surge Expands

U.S. commanders say additional fighters and bombers are being surged to sustain operations over Iran, including the capital region; timelines remain conditions-based. — Source: Air & Space Forces Magazine, Mar 9
