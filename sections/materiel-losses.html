Iran / IRGC Losses

NAVAL — Warships & patrol vessels destroyed
50+ Iranian naval vessels destroyed, including the Shahid Bagheri drone carrier and fast-attack craft. IRGC Navy and Islamic Republic of Iran Navy both operationally neutralised. CONFIRMED — Source: CENTCOM, Mar 10
AIR FORCE — Manned aircraft destroyed
~15 manned aircraft (F-4, Su-22 variants) confirmed destroyed on the ground; IRIAF runways and hardened aircraft shelters (HASs) struck; remaining fleet grounded. ESTIMATED — Source: ISW/CENTCOM, Mar 22
AIR DEFENCE — Batteries destroyed or suppressed
80+ air defence nodes struck; S-300 and Bavar-373 central coverage severely degraded, enabling stand-in strikes from early in the campaign. ESTIMATED — Source: CENTCOM/Cooper, Mar 4; ISW, Mar 6
MISSILES — Ballistic missile TELs eliminated
~20–30 transporter-erector-launchers (TELs) eliminated; convoys and tunnel access points targeted throughout the campaign. IDF assesses ~100–200 launchers may remain operational. US/ISRAELI CLAIM — Source: IDF/ISW, Mar 10–22
COMMAND & CONTROL — Leadership & C2 nodes struck
100+ C2 nodes, IRGC headquarters, ministry facilities and leadership sites struck. Supreme Leader Khamenei confirmed killed Mar 1; multiple generals killed; Quds Force liaisons targeted in Beirut. CONFIRMED — Source: CENTCOM; ISW/CT, Mar 6; multiple outlets
ENERGY & NUCLEAR — Sites struck
40+ energy and nuclear infrastructure sites struck, including Natanz, Isfahan, and Fordow (unconfirmed depth); five Iranian energy sites confirmed damaged per Iranian official statements; Kharg Island military facilities struck Mar 14. ESTIMATED — Source: IEA; Reuters; CNBC/IEA, Mar 23

US / Coalition Losses

AIRCRAFT — Lost (all causes)
A congressional report and subsequent media coverage on May 22–23 estimated that roughly 42 U.S. aircraft have been lost or damaged during the campaign, with unmanned systems accounting for the majority of those losses. ESTIMATED — Source: Defense News; Ynet; May 22–23
PERSONNEL — KIA / WIA
13 KIA / 140+ WIA. Six killed in a drone strike in Kuwait (early campaign); seventh KIA confirmed Mar 9; total reached 13 per Pentagon update Mar 15. WIA figures include blast injury and non-combat incidents. CONFIRMED — Source: Pentagon/NPR, Mar 15
COST — Estimated campaign expenditure
$29B estimated total campaign cost to date — Pentagon officials provided the updated figure on May 12, 2026, reflecting sustained operational, munitions, and logistics expenditures; analysts note this is a significant increase from earlier public estimates and could influence budgeting and political support for continued operations. CONFIRMED — Source: Reuters, May 12 Pentagon officials told Reuters the U.S. campaign in Iran had cost roughly $29 billion to date.
