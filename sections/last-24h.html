Nov 26 Italy and Kuwait said two Rheinmetall Italia technicians were killed and two people injured after Iranian drones targeted a vital facility in Kuwait on November 26. Kuwait said its forces intercepted several drones; Iran denied conducting an offensive operation in the area. The deaths are the first reported fatalities among foreign civilian defense contractors in the current Kuwait attacks and mark a major escalation beyond earlier nonfatal or unverified incidents. The casualties are reported by Italian and Kuwaiti authorities, but Iran disputes the attribution and the precise mechanism remains unclear. AP News
Nov 25 Security agents arrested activist and Oscar-nominated screenwriter Mehdi Mahmoudian at his Tehran home on November 25, according to friends, activists and Iranian reformist media. Reports said agents seized his electronic devices; authorities had not publicly given a reason by November 26. The arrest adds a new internal-repression case involving a prominent civil-society figure who campaigned for jailed protesters and against executions. It indicates continued pressure on dissenting voices during the war, although the legal basis, detention location and Mahmoudian’s condition remain unconfirmed. AP News
Nov 25 Iran’s Revolutionary Guards said on November 25 that two oil tankers struck naval mines, exploded and caught fire in the Strait of Hormuz, and warned vessels to coordinate passage with Iranian authorities. Reuters reported the claim, but no vessel identities, casualties, spill, matching UKMTO notice, or independent imagery were available. The claim would represent a further maritime escalation after the November 23 multi-vessel projectile incident and suggests Iran may be seeking to impose or demonstrate a controlled transit regime. However, the mine strikes, reported fires, number of vessels, and attribution remain unverified; it does not establish a formal closure of the Strait. Reuters via StreetInsider
Nov 23 UKMTO reported that at about 2025 UTC on November 23, three eastbound vessels in the Strait of Hormuz were struck by unknown projectiles. All continued their voyages and all crew were accounted for; damage and environmental effects were not yet known. This materially escalates the maritime crisis from repeated single-vessel attacks on November 20–21 to a multi-vessel incident inside the Strait. The attacker, weapons, damage severity, and whether the vessels were deliberately targeted as a group remain unconfirmed, and the report does not establish a formal waterway closure. UKMTO
Nov 22 JMIC said a bulk carrier anchored near Dibba, northeast of Khor Fakkan, was struck on its port side by an unknown projectile and caught fire on November 22. No casualties were reported; the incident was reportedly reported late on November 23. This adds a previously absent vessel attack west of the Strait of Hormuz, broadening the maritime escalation beyond the three-vessel incident already in the feed. JMIC also dates those tanker strikes to November 22, conflicting with the November 23 date in the UKMTO warning; attribution and damage remain unresolved. JMIC
