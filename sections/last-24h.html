Oct 13 Saudi Arabia carried out new airstrikes on Sanaa on October 13 after warning civilians near the Yemen Petroleum Company’s Sabah fuel-depot area to leave. Houthi-run media and residents reported the strikes, while the facility operator said the site held no petroleum stocks. The Houthis vowed retaliation. The strikes mark a new phase of direct Saudi-Houthi escalation, extending retaliation into energy-related infrastructure in the Houthi-held capital. The reported damage and any subsequent Houthi response remain only partly independently verified. Reuters via Investing.com
Oct 12 Shipping through the Strait of Hormuz nearly stopped over the weekend after a series of tanker attacks. Kpler data showed five commodity vessels transited on Saturday and none on Sunday, compared with 31 during the previous weekend. The data show that attacks are materially changing commercial behavior and sharply reducing traffic, even without a formal closure of the Strait. This adds an important measure of disruption beyond individual vessel incidents. Reuters via Gulf Times
Oct 12 UKMTO posted three additional attack notices covering a container vessel and two tankers struck by unknown projectiles during Strait of Hormuz transits. Authorities were investigating, and the notices did not attribute responsibility to Iran. The incidents expand the threat from tanker traffic to container shipping and show continued attacks after those already reported on October 10–11. They establish maritime danger but not Iranian responsibility. UKMTO
Oct 12 Iran said it was still reviewing the U.S. response to Tehran’s proposal for reopening the Strait of Hormuz and would reply through intermediaries. Iranian officials said the response did not adequately address Iran’s conditions. Indirect diplomacy remains active but has produced no reopening agreement or timetable. The nuclear issue is a central obstacle, with Washington seeking to address it first while Tehran prioritizes maritime reopening. Associated Press
Oct 11 UKMTO reported further attacks on October 10–11, including an outbound tanker hit by a projectile in the Strait and another tanker struck near Sohar anchorage, Oman. Fires or damage were reported, but crews were safe and the incidents were not attributed to Iran. The attacks demonstrate sustained maritime disruption after the earlier traffic collapse and show that the threat extends beyond the Strait into adjacent Omani waters. They establish incidents, not responsibility. UKMTO
