Sep 16 Saudi Arabia accused Houthi forces of attempting to attack Mecca on September 16 and called any such attack a “red line.” The Houthis denied responsibility; Saudi Arabia has not publicly established the projectile’s origin, and no independently verified impact in Mecca was reported. An alleged threat to Mecca raises the political stakes of the Houthi-Saudi conflict beyond attacks on energy and military targets, potentially increasing pressure on Riyadh to retaliate. Attribution and physical effects remain unconfirmed. Associated Press
Sep 15 U.S. Central Command said U.S. forces destroyed two small Iranian boats off Iran on September 15 after they attempted to seize a U.S. unmanned surface vessel. Iranian media separately reported that two fishing boats were attacked in Hormozgan and several fishermen were missing; it is unclear whether the accounts describe the same incident. The reported encounter marks a direct U.S.-Iran maritime exchange after the September 13 vessel strike near Qeshm Island, indicating that fighting around Iranian waters has continued. The boats’ identities and the fishermen’s fate remain unresolved. Associated Press
Sep 15 Saudi Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman met Egyptian President Abdel Fattah el-Sissi on September 15 as Houthi attacks continued against Saudi shipping and infrastructure. The talks followed reported Houthi gains near Bab el-Mandeb and the shutdown of Saudi Arabia’s East-West oil pipeline. Riyadh’s outreach shows the crisis is prompting broader regional political coordination across both maritime approaches to the Gulf. The meeting does not establish that Egypt agreed to military participation. Associated Press
Sep 14 Iran-aligned Houthi forces launched a new wave of attacks against Saudi targets on September 14, including a claimed missile-and-drone strike on Khamis Mushait air base. The Saudi-led coalition reported 13 civilians wounded, while damage to the base was not independently verified. The attacks show renewed Houthi pressure on Saudi Arabia alongside reported territorial consolidation near Bab el-Mandeb, widening the active regional and maritime security crisis beyond the Strait of Hormuz. Associated Press
Sep 14 Houthi forces said they seized the Greater and Lesser Hanish islands in the southern Red Sea. Saudi-backed Yemeni forces said they were trying to regain territory around Bab el-Mandeb, but the reported seizure has not been independently verified. If confirmed, the move would extend Houthi-controlled territory toward Bab el-Mandeb and add pressure to the Red Sea route, which has become more important as shipping around Hormuz remains constrained. Associated Press
