Oct 12 Iran said it was still reviewing the U.S. response to Tehran’s proposal for reopening the Strait of Hormuz and would reply through intermediaries. Iranian officials said the response did not adequately address Iran’s conditions. Indirect diplomacy remains active but has produced no reopening agreement or timetable. The nuclear issue is a central obstacle, with Washington seeking to address it first while Tehran prioritizes maritime reopening. Associated Press
Oct 11 UKMTO reported further attacks on October 10–11, including an outbound tanker hit by a projectile in the Strait and another tanker struck near Sohar anchorage, Oman. Fires or damage were reported, but crews were safe and the incidents were not attributed to Iran. The attacks demonstrate sustained maritime disruption after the earlier traffic collapse and show that the threat extends beyond the Strait into adjacent Omani waters. They establish incidents, not responsibility. UKMTO
Oct 10 A projectile attack on King Khalid International Airport on October 10 killed at least 12 people and wounded more than 300, according to Reuters. Saudi authorities blamed Iran-backed Houthis, though responsibility was not independently confirmed. A further projectile struck the airport passenger terminal on October 11. The mass-casualty attack is the most serious Saudi incident in more than a decade and raises pressure for direct U.S. participation in Saudi operations against the Houthis. Continued attacks also complicate efforts to contain the regional war. Reuters via MarketScreener
Oct 8 President Donald Trump said the United States would not resume military strikes on Iran before the November 3 midterm elections. AP reported that Washington believes it is gaining leverage by helping tankers transit Hormuz while restricting Iran’s oil exports. The statement places a new publicly declared political constraint on near-term U.S. escalation while leaving maritime pressure and economic coercion in place. It is political guidance, not a permanent renunciation of military action. Associated Press
Oct 7 Yemen’s Iran-backed Houthis claimed new attacks on Saudi military facilities and airports. The Associated Press did not independently verify the targets or any resulting damage. The claims indicate further regional spillover and could increase risks around Saudi Arabia and the Red Sea’s southern approaches, but they do not establish confirmed strikes or damage. Associated Press
