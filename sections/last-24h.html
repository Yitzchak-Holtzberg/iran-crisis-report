Nov 20 U.S. envoys Steve Witkoff and Jared Kushner met Qatari mediators to discuss efforts to revive a ceasefire, restart nuclear negotiations and prevent a return to open conflict. No agreement or ceasefire was announced. The meeting shows senior-level mediation remains active despite renewed attacks, but it produced no publicly reported mechanism to halt strikes, reopen maritime traffic or restore nuclear-inspection access. It is a new diplomatic contact, not evidence of a breakthrough. Axios
Nov 19 Reuters reported that shipping through the Strait of Hormuz slowed after attacks on tankers while U.S.–Iran talks stalled. The report described a continued reduction in commercial traffic but did not establish that the strait was formally closed. The reported slowdown adds a measurable operational effect to the maritime crisis: disruption is affecting transit volumes, not merely producing isolated incident reports. Iranian claims that additional tankers struck mines remain independently unconfirmed. Reuters via Gulf Times
Nov 19 Axios reported that President Trump remained open to a possible joint U.S.-Israeli attack on Iran and that the White House had sought assessments of possible Iranian retaliation against Gulf oil and gas infrastructure. No new strike was reported, and the article said an attack was not imminent. The report shows that renewed large-scale U.S. action remains under consideration after the recent exchange, keeping escalation risks elevated. It is a planning and policy development, not evidence that a new operation has begun. Axios
Nov 18 UKMTO/JMIC maintained a severe threat level across the Gulf, Strait of Hormuz, Gulf of Oman and North Arabian Sea. Its advisory cited sustained naval presence, increased IRGC hailing and monitoring, possible attacks on AIS-transmitting vessels and congestion near anchorages. The official advisory confirms that maritime conditions remain operationally dangerous after the recent attacks and reinforces the shipping-disruption picture. It does not announce a new confirmed strike or formal legal closure of the Strait of Hormuz. UKMTO/JMIC
Nov 18 Iran targeted the U.S.-used Ali Al Salem air base in Kuwait and launched several attacks toward Bahrain, the Associated Press reported. Kuwait and Bahrain said their air defenses intercepted the attacks and reported no immediate damage or casualties. The retaliation cycle has widened geographically beyond Bahrain to a second Gulf state and another U.S.-linked facility. Interceptions were reported, but damage at the alleged targets remains unconfirmed. Associated Press
