Nov 16 UKMTO reported that Indian shipping authorities alerted it to a tanker attack in the Strait of Hormuz. An unknown projectile caused an engine-room fire; no casualties were reported, and the vessel, attacker and final damage assessment were not identified. This is a newly documented attack in the Strait itself, distinct from the Nov. 11 incident near Ras al-Khaimah and unverified Iranian mine claims. It shows the maritime threat remained active in the waterway’s core, though responsibility and the full operational impact remain unknown. UKMTO
Nov 16 The USS Theodore Roosevelt carrier strike group arrived in the Middle East, bringing the number of U.S. aircraft carriers in the region to three, according to the Associated Press. The deployment adds substantial personnel and air and maritime capacity. The arrival materially strengthens the U.S. military’s options around the Iran confrontation and raises the potential consequences of further escalation. It does not by itself indicate that Washington has decided to resume strikes, as diplomatic efforts remained stalled. Associated Press
Nov 15 Bahrain said its air defenses intercepted and destroyed several Iranian aerial attacks before they reached their targets. Iran’s Revolutionary Guard separately claimed that six drones targeted a U.S. radar installation at Bahrain’s Riffa air base; the alleged strike was not independently verified. This is a second reported Iranian attack attempt against Bahrain after the Nov. 13 interception already in the feed, indicating renewed pressure rather than an isolated exchange. The supported effect is the Bahraini interception, not damage to the radar site. Reuters via StreetInsider
Nov 14 Iranian state television reported that three oil tankers hit Iranian naval mines and caught fire around the Strait of Hormuz. The vessels were not identified, and no independent confirmation of the incidents, casualties, damage, environmental effects or responsibility for the mines was available. If confirmed, the report would mark a significant maritime escalation beyond the projectile strikes already recorded on Nov. 10–11 and could further endanger shipping through the Strait. At present, it remains an Iranian claim without corroborating UKMTO, JMIC or other independent evidence. Associated Press
Nov 13 The U.S. military said it intercepted two Iranian attack drones headed toward Bahrain. Iran’s Revolutionary Guard claimed it struck a U.S. early-warning radar system in Bahrain after a U.S. strike in southern Iran; Washington reported no injuries or damage, and the alleged radar strike was not independently verified. This marks renewed kinetic exchange after several quieter days and the first reported Iranian attempt to target a facility in Bahrain since September. The verified effect is the interception of two drones, while the claimed Iranian retaliation remains unconfirmed. Associated Press
