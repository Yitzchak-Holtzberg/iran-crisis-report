Oct 20 The United Kingdom said it will deploy RAF Typhoon fighters for defensive counter-air operations over Saudi Arabia against Houthi missiles and drones. Britain also announced a military transport aircraft, additional advisers and a 90-day extension of Saudi aerial-refueling support; the jets are not authorized to strike Houthi ground targets. This is the clearest new external military commitment to Saudi Arabia since the renewed Houthi-Saudi escalation began. It widens the conflict’s international footprint and raises the risk of direct Western involvement while stopping short of offensive action. Associated Press
Oct 19 Yemeni officials said the death toll from the October 19 Houthi ballistic-missile strike on the al-Alam military training camp in Shabwa rose to 73 government recruits, with others missing and about 200 wounded. The Houthis acknowledged targeting the camp, but the figures were not independently verified. The reported toll makes this the deadliest single attack in the renewed Yemen fighting and marks a major escalation against Saudi-backed government forces beyond the earlier strikes on Aden and Saudi-linked infrastructure. Associated Press
Oct 19 Houthi forces struck a Yemeni government military training camp in Shabwa with ballistic missiles, according to Yemeni military and provincial officials. The Houthis acknowledged targeting the camp; initial reports said at least 53 people were killed and about 200 wounded before the reported toll rose to 73. The attack marks a sharp escalation in the renewed Yemen conflict by hitting a concentrated military facility and causing the largest reported single-day military casualty count in the current fighting. Associated Press
Oct 17 Iran’s Revolutionary Guard said an oil supertanker carrying more than two million barrels of crude exploded and caught fire after striking a naval mine while using an unauthorized route through the Strait of Hormuz. The vessel, cause, crew status and mine involvement were not independently verified. If confirmed, the incident would be more serious than the October 14 reported cargo-ship attacks and would indicate further escalation against commercial shipping. It remains an Iranian claim without matching independent confirmation. Associated Press
Oct 16 Two missiles struck Aden International Airport, according to Yemeni officials cited by the Associated Press. Houthi sources said they targeted a nearby Saudi-led coalition command facility and weapons depot; no casualties were reported. The attack extends the Houthi-Saudi and Yemeni-government exchange to another strategic transport facility after the October 15 reported presidential-palace strike. The claimed target and attribution remain contested. Associated Press
