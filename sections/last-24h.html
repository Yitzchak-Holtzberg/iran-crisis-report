EVACUATION — UK WITHDRAWS AMBASSADOR AND ALL EMBASSY STAFF FROM TEHRAN
The British government announced Friday that it has withdrawn its ambassador and all embassy staff from Tehran, citing "ongoing security concerns." The UK Foreign Office issued an emergency travel advisory urging British nationals in Iran to leave immediately, warning that consular support is now "extremely limited" with the embassy operating remotely only. The UK joins a growing list of nations pulling staff: China advised its nationals to evacuate Iran "as soon as possible" — a dramatic signal given Beijing is Iran's largest oil customer and main arms supplier. Australia, India, South Korea, and Sweden have all upgraded travel advisories to "do not travel." The coordinated multi-nation departure is the most concrete signal yet that governments with intelligence access believe a US-Iran military confrontation is imminent. — Sources: The Independent, Sky News, Politico.eu, Daily Mail, The Telegraph, Feb 27
