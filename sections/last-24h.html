The diplomatic track has shifted from exchanging proposals toward an explicit risk of renewed combat, worsening the outlook for a ceasefire and reopening of the Strait of Hormuz. Associated Press

Sep 30 Iranian officials said Tehran received a formal U.S. response to its latest proposal to end the war and reopen the Strait of Hormuz. The contents were not disclosed, and there was no confirmation that Washington accepted Iran’s conditions. The exchange marks movement beyond the expected mediator meetings, but it does not establish an agreement, ceasefire, sanctions relief or verified reopening of the strait. AP
Sep 29 The United States sanctioned 10 individuals and entities in Iran, Hong Kong and Pakistan that it accused of helping procure weapons and components for Iran’s defense ministry. AP also reported that Iran’s currency fell beyond 2.5 million rials per U.S. dollar. The measures add economic pressure and target Iran’s defense supply chain while diplomacy remains unsettled, potentially increasing the difficulty of reaching a broader compromise. AP
