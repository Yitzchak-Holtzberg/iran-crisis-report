Nov 11 JMIC/UKMTO identified the Nov. 11 suspicious maritime incident as the tanker Dominica Prosperity. It was reportedly struck by an unknown projectile in the engine room about 14 nautical miles northwest of Ras al-Khaimah, UAE. No casualties or pollution were reported, and attribution remains unknown. This materially upgrades the Nov. 11 incident from unexplained suspicious activity to a reported projectile strike involving a named tanker. It confirms that commercial shipping remains exposed near the Strait while leaving the weapon’s origin, attacker and full damage assessment unresolved. JMIC/UKMTO
Nov 11 UKMTO reported suspicious activity involving a tanker in the Strait of Hormuz. Crew status, vessel damage and environmental effects were unknown when the warning was issued; authorities were investigating and vessels were advised to transit cautiously. UKMTO did not classify it as an attack or identify a responsible party. This is a new security incident at the waterway’s core, following several recent attacks and dangerous encounters around the Strait. It indicates that operational uncertainty remains high for commercial shipping, although the report does not establish that an attack occurred or who was responsible. UKMTO
Nov 10 UKMTO reported that a tanker was struck by an unknown projectile 19 nautical miles northeast of Lima, Oman. The crew were safe and no environmental impact was reported; the master was expected to assess damage at first light. Attribution was not established. The incident shows that maritime attacks or dangerous encounters remain active near the eastern approaches to the Strait of Hormuz despite ongoing diplomatic signaling. It broadens the recent pattern beyond the Strait itself while leaving responsibility unresolved. UKMTO
Nov 10 Secretary of State Marco Rubio urged other countries to help protect shipping in the Strait of Hormuz and said Washington had made no progress with G7 counterparts on the issue during talks in Paris. The United States is publicly seeking broader international participation in maritime security rather than relying solely on unilateral enforcement. The lack of G7 agreement underscores the unresolved diplomatic and operational risks around the waterway. Associated Press
Nov 9 Secretary of State Marco Rubio said negotiations with Iran were not near a breakthrough. President Trump nevertheless said renewed strikes were unlikely if talks produced a strong agreement, while warning that the United States had identified infrastructure targets if military action resumed. Diplomacy remains active but fragile. Washington is pairing conditional openness to negotiations with explicit preparation for renewed escalation, and there is still no reported agreement to reopen the Strait of Hormuz or restore nuclear inspections. Associated Press
