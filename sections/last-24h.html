Nov 4 UKMTO reported that a tanker was struck by two unidentified projectiles at about 2320 UTC on Nov. 4, roughly 50 nautical miles north of Madinat ash Shamal, Qatar. Crew status and damage were initially unknown, and no attribution was established. The incident extends reported maritime attacks beyond the immediate Strait of Hormuz approaches into the central Persian Gulf and toward Qatar’s energy-maritime zone, increasing risks to commercial shipping and regional energy flows. Associated Press
Nov 3 A U.S. official told the Associated Press that U.S. forces conducted “defensive” strikes against Iranian targets in or near the Strait of Hormuz after attacks and explosions involving shipping. Specific targets, damage and casualties were not independently confirmed. The reported strikes mark renewed U.S. use of force after a lull and occurred while mediators were pursuing a framework to reopen the waterway. They raise retaliation risks and could undermine the diplomatic track. Associated Press
Nov 1 Iranian state media reported that Tehran designated the British navy and air force as terrorist organizations and declared MI6 a hostile entity. Associated reports of an attack on fishing boats near Qeshm Island, including missing and wounded fishermen, were not independently verified. The designations mark a new political and legal escalation against the United Kingdom, potentially raising risks for British personnel and interests, although no attack on British facilities or forces was reported. Associated Press
Oct 31 Iranian negotiators met Qatari mediators in Doha on possible paths out of the war. The contacts occurred alongside Iranian military-readiness measures and Supreme Leader Ali Khamenei’s warning of an immediate response to a potential attack. No agreement or ceasefire was reported. The talks preserve a diplomatic channel but have not produced de-escalation, an accepted proposal, or restored nuclear inspections. Their overlap with readiness warnings underscores the continued risk of escalation. Reuters
Oct 30 Maritime security sources told Reuters that a small boat attached limpet mines to a tanker in the Strait of Hormuz, causing an engine-room fire and temporarily disabling the vessel. The report did not establish who placed the mines or provide an independent forensic assessment. If confirmed, the incident would mark a tactical shift from the projectile attacks already documented in the feed, demonstrating a covert close-hull attack capable of temporarily disabling a tanker. Attribution remains unresolved. Reuters
