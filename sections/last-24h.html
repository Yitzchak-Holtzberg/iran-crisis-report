Nov 23 UKMTO reported that at about 2025 UTC on November 23, three eastbound vessels in the Strait of Hormuz were struck by unknown projectiles. All continued their voyages and all crew were accounted for; damage and environmental effects were not yet known. This materially escalates the maritime crisis from repeated single-vessel attacks on November 20–21 to a multi-vessel incident inside the Strait. The attacker, weapons, damage severity, and whether the vessels were deliberately targeted as a group remain unconfirmed, and the report does not establish a formal waterway closure. UKMTO
Nov 22 UKMTO reported an incident at about 1425 UTC involving a tanker and military forces south or southeast of Chabahar, Iran. The advisory did not identify the military force, specify damage or casualties, or determine whether the vessel was attacked, intercepted, boarded, or compelled to stop. This is a new officially logged maritime incident after the November 20–21 tanker attacks, extending the area of concern eastward from the Khasab approaches into the Gulf of Oman. It raises uncertainty about military activity near Iran without independently establishing attribution or a formal closure of the waterway. UKMTO
Nov 21 Bahrain said an Iranian attack hit a civilian industrial facility on November 21. Iran’s Revolutionary Guards separately claimed that a missile-and-drone operation targeted U.S. positions at Al-Riffa and Awali in retaliation for alleged violations of Iranian airspace; the competing accounts were not reconciled. The reported facility impact adds a new Bahrain incident to the regional escalation after the earlier November fighting. Bahrain’s account is reported by Reuters, while Iran’s claim of successful attacks on U.S. bases remains unverified, so the event demonstrates continued regional retaliation without establishing the operation’s full effect. Reuters via Internazionale
Nov 21 UKMTO reported that an outbound tanker was struck in the engine room by an unknown projectile at about 0550 UTC on November 21, roughly 17 nautical miles east of Khasab, Oman. No casualties were reported and environmental effects remained unconfirmed. This is a confirmed kinetic maritime incident inside the Strait, following the November 20 tanker attack and the previously reported traffic slowdown. It materially raises shipping risks, although UKMTO did not identify the attacker or establish a formal closure of the waterway. UKMTO
Nov 20 UKMTO reported that an outbound tanker was struck on its port bow by an unknown projectile about eight nautical miles north of Khasab, Oman, at approximately 1500 UTC on November 20. The ship’s security officer reported a fire; no casualties or environmental impact were reported at the time. The incident adds an officially reported tanker attack to the maritime disruption already documented in the feed and establishes repeated attacks on successive days. The projectile’s source and the attacker’s identity remain unconfirmed, and no sinking or spill was reported. UKMTO
