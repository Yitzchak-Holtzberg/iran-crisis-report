Oct 22 Iranian state television and the Revolutionary Guard said seven oil tankers were damaged or caught fire in or near the Strait of Hormuz overnight. Tehran attributed the incidents to attacks or naval mines, but the number, causes, vessel identities and Iranian responsibility were not independently verified. If confirmed, this would be the largest reported single-day disruption to commercial shipping since the war began and a significant escalation beyond the previously reported October 17 supertanker incident. Associated Press
Oct 21 Saudi authorities said an attack on King Fahd International Airport in Dammam killed a Saudi citizen and an Indian national resident in Saudi Arabia. The attack occurred amid the wider Houthi campaign, but the projectile and precise attribution were not independently established. The fatal strike extends the renewed Houthi-Saudi escalation to a major civilian aviation facility, increasing risks to air travel, foreign personnel and critical infrastructure in eastern Saudi Arabia. Associated Press
Oct 21 The U.S. State Department ordered some diplomats and family members of U.S. government employees to depart Saudi Arabia amid heightened concerns over further Houthi attacks and regional escalation. The partial diplomatic drawdown is a concrete U.S. force-protection measure indicating that Washington considers the threat environment in Saudi Arabia to have deteriorated, even as Britain expands defensive air support. Associated Press
Oct 20 The United Kingdom said it will deploy RAF Typhoon fighters for defensive counter-air operations over Saudi Arabia against Houthi missiles and drones. Britain also announced a military transport aircraft, additional advisers and a 90-day extension of Saudi aerial-refueling support; the jets are not authorized to strike Houthi ground targets. This is the clearest new external military commitment to Saudi Arabia since the renewed Houthi-Saudi escalation began. It widens the conflict’s international footprint and raises the risk of direct Western involvement while stopping short of offensive action. Associated Press
Oct 19 Yemeni officials said the death toll from the October 19 Houthi ballistic-missile strike on the al-Alam military training camp in Shabwa rose to 73 government recruits, with others missing and about 200 wounded. The Houthis acknowledged targeting the camp, but the figures were not independently verified. The reported toll makes this the deadliest single attack in the renewed Yemen fighting and marks a major escalation against Saudi-backed government forces beyond the earlier strikes on Aden and Saudi-linked infrastructure. Associated Press
