NUCLEAR — IAEA REPORTS NEW UNDERGROUND ENRICHMENT SITE NEAR ISFAHAN

The finding represents a significant intelligence gap in assessing Iran’s nuclear programme status. — Source: IAEA/The National, Mar 18, 2026

ECONOMY — IRAQ RESUMES OIL EXPORTS VIA TURKEY AFTER HORMUZ CLOSURE

Iraq’s North Oil Company restarted the Sarlo pumping station to route Kirkuk oil through Kurdistan to the Ceyhan pipeline — bypassing Iranian-controlled waters. — Source: CBS News/AFP, Mar 18, 2026
