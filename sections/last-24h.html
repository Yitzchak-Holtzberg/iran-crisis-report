Sep 6 Iran claimed on Sept. 6 that it struck a U.S. unmanned military vessel in the Strait of Hormuz. The United States denied the claim and called it false. The allegation followed the previous day’s U.S. strikes on three Iranian oil tankers. Although no U.S. vessel damage or casualties were independently confirmed, the claim extends the reported tit-for-tat attacks directly into the Strait of Hormuz, where military activity already poses heightened risks to commercial shipping. Associated Press
Sep 5 Iranian state media said four U.S. missiles struck a tanker about 10 kilometers from Kharg Island on Sept. 5, with no casualties. The United States had not confirmed the allegation; AP said Iran provided no evidence, while Reuters separately reported explosions of unknown origin nearby. The allegation places a reported new strike near Iran’s principal oil-export hub and could increase risks to tanker operations and energy exports. The incident, its cause and any damage remain unverified. Associated Press
Sep 4 The United States, Britain, France and Germany sought support for an IAEA Board resolution referring Iran to the U.N. Security Council over safeguards and nonproliferation obligations. The draft remained under negotiation and had not yet been submitted or adopted. The initiative would escalate the nuclear dispute toward Security Council involvement while inspectors lack access to sites affected by earlier strikes and cannot verify Iran’s enriched-uranium stockpile, further narrowing diplomatic space. Associated Press
Sep 4 Associated Press reported that ship traffic through the Strait of Hormuz remained low several days after the renewed U.S.–Iran exchanges and subsequent Iranian attacks on regional targets. Persistent traffic suppression indicates an operational effect beyond individual attacks, increasing the risk of delays, rerouting, higher insurance costs and further energy-market disruption. Associated Press
Sep 3 Kuwait said its air defenses intercepted Iranian missiles and drones early Sept. 3. Iran said the attack targeted U.S. facilities in Kuwait; Kuwait reported no immediate casualties or confirmed damage, and successful strikes on U.S. bases were not independently verified. This adds Kuwait, which hosts major U.S. facilities, to the latest round of Iranian attacks on Gulf states and raises the risk of direct U.S.–Iran combat spreading across Gulf territory. Associated Press
