Thursday's Geneva talks may be the last diplomatic window

The Diplomatic Track

FEB 6 — Muscat, Oman
Round 1: Indirect talks mediated by Oman. Iran's FM Abbas Araghchi and US envoys Steve Witkoff & Jared Kushner establish initial contact.
FEB 17-18 — Geneva
Round 2: Both sides report "good progress" and agree on "guiding principles." Iran's FM Araghchi: "I believe we made good progress... we will not reach it quickly." Iranian delegation promised to return with detailed proposals within two weeks. JD Vance mixed review: "In some ways it went well. They agreed to meet afterwards. But the president has set some red lines the Iranians are not yet willing to acknowledge." Strait of Hormuz partially closed for IRGC live-fire drills while talks were underway.
FEB 17 — Tehran
Khamenei rejects terms: During IRGC drills in the Strait of Hormuz, warns the US Navy "could be sunk." Separately: "The US President says their army is the world's strongest, but the strongest army in the world can sometimes be slapped so hard it cannot get up."
FEB 20 — Washington
Trump: Strikes could begin "within 10 days" if no deal. Orders largest military surge since 2003. Iran sends letter to UN: "All bases, facilities and assets of the hostile force in the region would constitute legitimate targets." US military planning reportedly includes targeting individuals and possibly regime change (Reuters). Senior admin official: all forces in place by mid-March.
FEB 25 — State of the Union
Trump: "I will never allow the world's number one sponsor of terror to have a nuclear weapon." Claims 32,000 killed in protests. Iran's FM fires back: Trump told "big lies" — but says deal is "within reach."
FEB 27 (THU) — Geneva
Round 3: FM Araghchi departed Tehran confirmed for Switzerland. Araghchi pre-statement: "Iran will under no circumstances ever develop a nuclear weapon; neither will we Iranians ever forgo our right to harness the dividends of peaceful nuclear technology." Iran Parliament Speaker Ghalibaf: "If you choose the path of diplomacy — one in which Iranian dignity and mutual interests are respected — we will remain at the negotiating table. If not, you will once again taste the strong blow of the Iranian nation." Potentially the final diplomatic window — Trump's 10-day deadline expires early March.

The UK-US Rift

PM Starmer blocked the US from using RAF Fairford (B-52/B-2 bombers) and Diego Garcia. Legal basis: participation would breach international law. In retaliation, Trump withdrew support for the UK-Mauritius Chagos Islands deal.

However, Britain quietly deployed Typhoons and F-35Bs to Qatar and Cyprus — defensive assets in case of Iranian retaliation.

Israel: Finger on the Trigger

IDF at highest readiness since the June 2025 twelve-day war. 280,000 reservists authorized. IDF Chief: "prepared to employ unprecedented offensive power." Coordinating with CENTCOM; requesting advance warning before any US strikes. Jan 5 Security Cabinet strike authorization remains active.
