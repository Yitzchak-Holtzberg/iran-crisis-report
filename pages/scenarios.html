The Big Picture: Three Scenarios

Scenario 1: Deal

Iran accepts enrichment limits in Geneva. Sanctions eased. Military stands down. Likelihood: Low. Khamenei's rhetoric makes concessions politically impossible without losing face.

Scenario 2: Limited Strikes

US targets nuclear facilities. UK won't participate. Israel joins. Iran retaliates — Strait of Hormuz, proxy attacks. Khamenei's promise: "a regional war."

Scenario 3: Regime Collapse

Economic freefall + military pressure + internal protests = the regime fractures from within. IRGC power struggle. Pahlavi's "wide tent" offer to insiders becomes relevant. Unprecedented but no longer unthinkable.
