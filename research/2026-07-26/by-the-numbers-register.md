# By-the-numbers evidence register

Reviewed: 2026-07-26

Purpose: support the Atlas evidence desk with bounded, source-specific measurements. This register supplements the 2026-07-23 fact register; it does not relax its prohibitions on invented current inventories, false scenario probabilities, or undated humanitarian totals.

## Editorial rules

1. Every number keeps its observation or publication cutoff.
2. Belligerent and government estimates remain attributed even when a research institution cites them.
3. Known launches or losses are never subtracted from an uncertain starting inventory to invent a current remainder.
4. “No current accounting” is a state of knowledge, not a numeric zero.
5. Theoretical nuclear weapon equivalents are not weapons, warheads, or evidence of a completed weaponization chain.
6. Diaspora turnout is evidence of diaspora mobilization, not representative support inside Iran.
7. Hormuz is measured across volume, routes, insurance, crews, ports, and political restraint—not as a binary open/closed label.

## Accepted measurements

### Military campaign

- **6,770 launches through 1 April.** CSIS reports combined Iranian missile and drone launches. Use as campaign expenditure only. Do not infer the surviving inventory.
  - Source: https://www.csis.org/analysis/preventing-irans-military-reconstitution
- **Nearly all conventional gray-hull surface combatants lost.** CSIS bases this on satellite imagery. The claim does not cover all small craft, mines, coastal missiles, drones, or undersea systems.
  - Source: https://www.csis.org/analysis/preventing-irans-military-reconstitution
- **70% missile stock and 40% drone stock reportedly retained.** These are U.S. estimates cited by CSIS and remain attributed rather than independently verified.
  - Source: https://www.csis.org/analysis/preventing-irans-military-reconstitution
- **40% of the Bushehr Naval Base harbor basin destroyed.** Imagery-based estimate; useful for port damage, not total residual maritime capability.
  - Source: https://www.csis.org/analysis/preventing-irans-military-reconstitution
- **Launch tempo fell roughly 90% in the first week.** RAND reports a fall from more than 400 on day one to roughly 20–40 per day. Use as evidence of suppression, not exhaustion or capitulation.
  - Source: https://www.rand.org/pubs/commentary/2026/04/trumps-iran-war-is-a-dilemma-not-a-debacle.html

### Regional system and Hormuz

- **More than 4,000 projectiles directed at all six GCC states by 19 March.** Establishes region-wide exposure; does not measure a uniform impact or damage rate.
  - Source: https://www.iiss.org/online-analysis/online-analysis/2026/03/mapping-the-damage-iranian-strikes-on-the-gcc/
- **16.1 million barrels per day in June Gulf oil exports, including bypass routes, versus a 24 mb/d prewar average.** A documented partial recovery, not normalization.
  - Source: https://www.iea.org/reports/oil-market-report-july-2026
- **More than 70% of Gulf food imports reportedly pass through Hormuz.** Carnegie cites Reuters. Use as a structural exposure measure, not a loss rate.
  - Source: https://carnegieendowment.org/emissary/2026/04/iran-war-stress-test-gulf-states
- **Functional reopening requires commercial confidence.** RUSI distinguishes physical passage from normalized shipping, insurance, crewing, and risk.
  - Source: https://www.rusi.org/explore-our-research/publications/commentary/strait-hormuz-problem-what-securing-waterway-actually-requires

### Nuclear and diplomacy

- **440.9 kg enriched up to 60% at the last public accounting.** This is a dated baseline, not a current verified stockpile.
  - Source: https://apnews.com/article/d66a8ab6147e43bc7f544c76e063db80
- **Up to ten theoretical bomb equivalents if further enriched and weaponized.** Use only with the explicit caveat that this does not mean Iran has ten bombs or a proven weaponization chain.
  - Source: https://apnews.com/article/d66a8ab6147e43bc7f544c76e063db80
- **Four declared enrichment facilities.** Current status and material accounting remain incomplete without access.
  - Source: https://apnews.com/article/ccf574a324504b985f4b158f9d3d6941
- **Pickaxe Mountain has never been inspected and its contents remain unknown.** Continued excavation does not by itself establish nuclear material or activity.
  - Source: https://apnews.com/article/iran-us-war-pickaxe-mountain-nuclear-underground-what-to-know-2df2cbdbab7e21ba26e41cacdfa84e7c
- **JCPOA baselines:** 300 kg stockpile cap, 3.67% enrichment ceiling, roughly 75% enrichment-capacity reduction, roughly 90% stockpile reduction, and at least one year target breakout time.
  - Source: https://www.eeas.europa.eu/eeas/nuclear-agreement-%E2%80%93-jcpoa_en
  - Source: https://main.un.org/securitycouncil/en/content/2231/background

### Inside Iran

- **At least 3,375 civilian deaths and 32,314 injuries through OCHA’s early-May cutoff.** Time-bounded and partly dependent on Iranian authority reporting.
  - Source: https://iran.un.org/en/download/213204/319856
- **More than 115,000 civilian units and 763 schools damaged by 1 April.** Early-war snapshot, not a current cumulative total.
  - Source: https://iran.un.org/en/313395-islamic-republic-iran-humanitarian-update-no-02-3-april-2026
- **An 88-day national internet blackout affecting roughly 90 million people.** Relevant to civilian access and the limits of measuring political attitudes.
  - Source: https://apnews.com/article/iran-war-internet-shutdown-restored-a9a473245d9c6a6fc41822d844847c17
- **Estimated damage to 229 health centers and 49 hospitals.** Facility counts do not capture all staff, supply, power, communications, or access effects.
  - Source: https://iran.un.org/en

### Opposition

- **Police estimates of 250,000 at a Munich rally and 350,000 at a Toronto rally.** Evidence of large diaspora mobilization only.
  - Source: https://apnews.com/article/iran-munich-protests-reza-pahlavi-53d2448dd26d28a1206ecefdfde168f2
- **Five Iranian Kurdish groups formed a coalition.** Evidence of organizational coordination within one political field, not unified national leadership.
  - Source: https://apnews.com/article/6beae57e9fdc3546a61ec8f1432eef4b
- **No representative current polling inside Iran.** This is an admitted data gap and should be displayed as such.

## Expert convergence and disagreement

### Convergence

- Iran suffered severe conventional military damage.
- Residual distributed systems and technical knowledge remain strategically important.
- Operational access does not itself settle war aims, nuclear verification, commercial confidence, or political legitimacy.
- A pause is easier to reach than a durable settlement.

### Disagreement

- CSIS and parts of the U.S. policy debate put more weight on sustained reconstitution denial.
- RAND distinguishes clear operational progress from an unresolved termination dilemma.
- Chatham House puts more weight on the failure to achieve political objectives and the likelihood of renewed attrition.
- RUSI emphasizes that commercial normalization in Hormuz cannot be reduced to military passage.

## Quarantined formulations

- “92% of Iran’s navy destroyed” without a specific attributed scope.
- Any exact current missile, drone, launcher, or mine inventory.
- Any scenario percentage.
- A current casualty total extrapolated from dated OCHA snapshots.
- “Hormuz is open” or “Hormuz is closed” without a stated metric.
- “Iran has ten bombs.”
- “Zero nuclear material” or “zero nuclear activity” inferred from damage or missing access.
