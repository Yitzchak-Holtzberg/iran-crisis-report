# Canonical Fact Register

As of: 2026-07-23 ET  
Status: gated; no claim is admitted solely because it appears in the existing report or an institutional analysis

## Admission standard

For each consequential claim record:

- claim;
- event date;
- status: confirmed, reported-unconfirmed, disputed, assessment, or forecast;
- best direct evidence;
- corroborating evidence;
- conflicting evidence;
- source perspective;
- last checked;
- pages using the claim;
- and expiry or recheck condition.

## High-risk claim groups

### Leadership and governance

Pending primary and wire verification.

### Military casualties and civilian casualties

Pending source-by-source reconciliation and methodology review.

### Missile and drone launches

Pending reconciliation of CENTCOM, Israeli, Iranian, IISS, CTP–ISW, and independent estimates.

### Missile and drone stockpiles

Pending. Do not infer remaining inventory by subtracting claimed launches from prewar estimates without accounting for destruction, production, failures, decoys, and category differences.

### Naval losses

Pending independent confirmation. Distinguish IRIN, IRGC Navy, conventional combatants, auxiliaries, patrol craft, fast boats, and vessels damaged versus destroyed.

### Nuclear facilities and material

Pending IAEA-first review. Distinguish physical damage, enrichment capacity, material inventory, inspection access, weaponization knowledge, and policy decision.

### Strait of Hormuz traffic

Pending date-specific shipping data. Never use “closed,” “open,” “controlled,” or “normal” without defining the metric and time window.

### Oil, LNG, and product flows

Pending EIA, IEA, OPEC, shipping, and market-data review.

### U.S. and allied force posture

Pending official deployment records and specialist confirmation.

### Internal Iranian political and social conditions

Pending multi-source review that accounts for censorship, blackout conditions, selection bias, state propaganda, opposition incentives, and lack of representative polling.

### Negotiation terms

Pending direct text and government statements. Leaked proposals and institutional summaries must remain attributed.

## Rejected or quarantined claims

| Claim | Reason |
|---|---|
| “50+ Iranian vessels destroyed; both navies neutralized” | Aggregate is a belligerent claim and “neutralized” is contradicted by continued maritime attacks |
| “1,500–2,500 ballistic/cruise missiles remain” | Cannot be reconstructed from public evidence |
| “100–200 TELs remain” | Old or belligerent estimate presented without category, cutoff, or confidence |
| “Shahed production is 400 per day” | Unverified and highly definition-dependent |
| “Cyber capability is fully intact” | Absence of visible damage does not establish access, command, or effect |
| “Fordow's deepest halls are likely intact” | Physical modeling does not establish current contents or function |
| “Iran is withholding Fattah-1 as a strategic reserve” | Non-use is not evidence of a deliberate reserve |
| “The Iranian navy is eliminated but mines remain” | Conflates platforms with the whole maritime system |
| Any exact current scenario probability | No reproducible method |

## Cross-page rule

Every reader-facing repetition of a registered fact must use the same value, date, scope, and uncertainty language unless the page explicitly explains why a different measure is being used.
