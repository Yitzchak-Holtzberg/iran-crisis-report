These facilities are at 80–100m depth — at or beyond the weapon's effective range. Commercial satellite imagery shows crater signatures near entry shafts, but subsurface destruction is not assessable from surface imagery. A US intelligence assessment leaked to CSIS indicates strikes likely only set the programme back by months, not years — echoing the June 2025 assessment. Iran was observed sealing underground facility entrances at Isfahan with dump trucks and heavy equipment, suggesting some infrastructure survived. Whether Iran's nuclear programme was meaningfully degraded remains disputed. — Sources: CSIS Nuclear Network; FDD analysis, Mar 5; Reuters, Mar 2; BDA pending
