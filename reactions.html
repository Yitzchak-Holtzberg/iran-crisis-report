Mar 22 — Wide‑scale strikes reported across Iran; Natanz hit; IAEA investigating

The latest wave of strikes on Mar 22 targeted missile production and defence‑industrial sites across Iran. Regional reporting and Iranian state outlets described damage at the Natanz enrichment complex; the IAEA said it is investigating and reported no detected rise in radiation levels outside the facility. Iranian ballistic missiles struck towns near Dimona and Arad, causing dozens of injuries in southern Israel.
