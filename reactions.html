Iran: Strike Damage Assessment

Operation Epic Fury — confirmed and assessed damage to Iranian targets (Feb 28–Mar 1, 2026)
Critical
Supreme Leader Khamenei Reported Dead — Mar 1, 2026

Iranian state media confirmed Ayatollah Ali Khamenei is dead. Israeli officials publicly claimed he was killed during strikes on his command bunker in Tehran. NBC News separately reported his daughter, son-in-law and a grandchild were also killed. Both claims remain unverified by independent media due to communications blackout. — AP, Reuters, NBC News, Mar 1

💥 Nuclear & Missile Sites

  • Fordow — underground enrichment facility struck; bunker-busters used (CSIS)
  • Natanz — above-ground centrifuge halls destroyed; underground status unclear
  • Isfahan — uranium conversion plant hit; adjacent residential damage reported
  • Parchin — high-explosives test site struck; significant damage assessed
  • Shahrud — ballistic missile production facility struck
  • Imam Ali missile base (Kermanshah) — launch infrastructure destroyed

Sources: CSIS, ISW, JINSA, Army Recognition, Feb 28–Mar 1

🏢 Command & Control Targets

  • IRGC HQ, Tehran — struck by Israel; significant damage reported (The War Zone)
  • Ministry of Defense, Tehran — hit in second wave of strikes
  • IRGC Aerospace Force HQ — command node destroyed
  • Khamenei command bunker — targeted in coordinated strike; Khamenei status confirmed dead
  • State media broadcast centers — IRIB TV knocked offline for 4+ hours
  • Cyber infrastructure — major disruptions to Iranian internet and comms (Cloudflare)

Sources: The War Zone, Kurdistan24, Times Now, Feb 28

💀 Iranian Civilian Casualties

Al Jazeera reported two schools struck in Iran with 80+ killed; the claim is unverified by Western outlets. Reuters and CNN described "significant damage and heavy casualties in Tehran" while noting independent verification remains limited due to communications disruptions.

Iran's official casualty figures have not been released. Iranian state media initially went offline before resuming with official statements attributing civilian deaths to "American-Zionist aggression."

Gulf States: Iranian Retaliation Damage

Iran's ballistic missile barrage struck US-hosting Gulf nations — Feb 28, 2026
Active
Iran fired ~170 ballistic missiles at Israel and US bases across the Gulf

Iran confirmed launching approximately 170 ballistic missiles targeting Israel and US military installations across Gulf Arab states. THAAD, Patriot, Arrow 3, and Iron Dome batteries were activated simultaneously across the region. Multiple intercept failures were reported. — Critical Threats / The Guardian, Mar 1

🇧🇭 Bahrain — US 5th Fleet Base Hit

US Naval Support Activity Bahrain — home of the US 5th Fleet — was struck by Iranian ballistic missiles. Bahrain confirmed the attack on the base, which serves as the primary US naval command for the Persian Gulf. Casualties and damage extent at the facility were not immediately confirmed.

Bahrain's official response: Condemned the strikes as "a flagrant attack on Bahraini sovereignty and a direct assault on regional security."

Sources: Middle East Eye, Al Jazeera, Feb 28

🇦🇪 UAE — Abu Dhabi Casualty Confirmed

An Iranian missile struck near a US military installation in Abu Dhabi. One person was killed in Abu Dhabi and others were injured in separate incidents across the UAE. The UAE activated its Patriot and THAAD batteries; several intercepts were confirmed.

UAE's official response: Issued a formal protest to Iran and called an emergency session of the National Security Council. Abu Dhabi airport was temporarily closed.

Sources: US News & World Report, Al Jazeera, Feb 28

🇶🇦 Qatar — Al Udeid Air Base Targeted

Al Udeid Air Base — the largest US air base in the Middle East and CENTCOM Forward Headquarters — was among targets in Iran's barrage. Qatar confirmed missile activity over its territory. Patriot systems activated; initial reports indicated intercepts. Damage to the base was not confirmed by US military as of March 1.

Qatar's official response: Called for immediate de-escalation and offered to mediate. The Qatari Foreign Ministry summoned the Iranian ambassador.

Sources: Al Jazeera, Middle East Eye, Feb 28

🇦🇮 Saudi Arabia — Air Defense Activated

Saudi Arabia activated its Patriot missile batteries as Iranian projectiles were tracked over Saudi airspace. No confirmed impacts on Saudi soil were reported; intercepts occurred over the Red Sea and Gulf of Aden approach vectors. Prince Sultan Air Base near Riyadh went to full alert status.

Saudi Arabia's official response: Condemned "Iranian aggression" and coordinated air defense with US CENTCOM in real time. Convened emergency Gulf Cooperation Council (GCC) meeting.

Sources: Al Jazeera, BBC, Feb 28

🇰🇼 Kuwait — Ali Al Salem Base on Alert

Ali Al Salem Air Base, which hosts US Air Force aircraft including F/A-18s, was placed on Force Protection Condition Delta (highest threat level). Missile alerts were issued. No confirmed impacts in Kuwait were reported. Kuwait temporarily closed airspace to commercial traffic.

Kuwait's official response: Urged "maximum restraint from all parties" and offered humanitarian assistance for the region.

Israel: Strike Outcomes & Response

Israeli military assessment and public reaction to Operation Epic Fury

✈ Military Assessment

  • IDF declared the nuclear and ballistic missile threat "significantly degraded"
  • Arrow 3 and Iron Dome systems intercepted the majority of Iranian ballistic missiles
  • Some missiles penetrated defenses; limited damage reported in the Negev and near Eilat
  • Israel Air Force flew 200+ sorties in the first 24 hours
  • IDF confirmed use of F-35Is (Adir) and F-15Is (Ra'am) in strike packages

📣 Official Israeli Response

Prime Minister's office issued a statement: "Israel will not allow a nuclear Iran. We acted in pre-emptive self-defense. The operation continues until its objectives are achieved."

Defense Minister declared the strikes a "historic and necessary operation" and confirmed Israel was working in "full coordination" with the United States under Operation Epic Fury.

The Knesset convened an emergency session; cross-party support was expressed for the operation.

🏠 Israeli Home Front — Iranian Retaliation Impact

Iran's ~170-missile barrage against Israel triggered nationwide alerts. Arrow 3, David's Sling, and Iron Dome intercepted the majority of incoming missiles. Several missiles penetrated air defenses, with impacts reported near Be'er Sheva, the Negev desert, and areas around Eilat. Israeli officials confirmed casualties — the IDF reported 9 killed and 47 wounded, primarily in the Negev and Eilat regions where intercept coverage was thinner. Israel's Home Front Command expanded the emergency zone to the entire south.

Global Diplomatic Response

International reactions to Operation Epic Fury — Mar 1, 2026

🇺🇸 United States

President Trump confirmed "major combat operations" have begun against Iran. Trump stated he was "not happy" with the nuclear talks outcome that preceded the strikes, framing the operation as a last resort after diplomacy failed. He warned of "possible US casualties" and placed all US forces in the region on highest readiness. CENTCOM issued a public statement naming the campaign "Operation Epic Fury."

🇷🇺 Russia

Russia condemned the strikes as "a gross violation of international law and the UN Charter" and called an emergency UN Security Council session. Foreign Minister Lavrov called the operation "an act of aggression" and demanded immediate cessation. Russia warned of "unpredictable consequences for global security." No direct military action indicated.

🇨🇳 China

China issued a strongly-worded statement calling for "immediate cessation of military activities" and respect for Iran's sovereignty. The Foreign Ministry said China "firmly opposes" the strikes. China called for an emergency meeting of the UN Security Council alongside Russia. No indication of direct military involvement.

🇬🇧 United Kingdom

The UK government said it was "monitoring the situation closely" and had been briefed by US counterparts in advance of the strikes. The UK stopped short of endorsing the operation but called Iran's nuclear program "an existential threat to regional security." HMS Diamond (Type 45 destroyer) in the region was placed on heightened readiness.

🇫🇷 France & 🇩🇪 Germany

France and Germany jointly called for "maximum restraint and immediate de-escalation." Both nations condemned Iran's retaliation strikes on Gulf states as "unacceptable." The EU convened an emergency foreign affairs council. European states began evacuating nationals from Iran and neighboring countries.

🇹🇷 Turkey

Turkey condemned the strikes and offered to mediate between all parties. President Erdoğan called the operation "dangerous and destabilizing" and offered Ankara as a venue for emergency talks. Turkey closed its airspace to military overflights related to the operation and put its air force on alert along the Iranian border.

🌌 UN Security Council — Emergency Session

Russia and China jointly called an emergency UN Security Council session within hours of the strikes commencing. The US exercised its veto on a draft resolution demanding "immediate cessation of hostilities." The UK abstained; France voted in favor of the resolution. UN Secretary-General António Guterres called the situation "the most dangerous escalation in the Middle East in decades" and urged all parties to return to negotiations.
